What we do

Business Guy LLC designs, builds, and operates software products that help small businesses and real-estate professionals communicate with their customers and manage their workflows. Our products include automated deal-intake and email tools for real-estate investors, and Service Blue, an AI phone receptionist for home-service businesses, operated under our registered trade name.

Our applications and your data

Some of our applications connect to third-party services — including Google services — with the account holder's explicit authorization, in order to send and organize email and manage business workflows on that user's behalf. We access only the data each application needs to do its job, we do not sell personal data, and our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
